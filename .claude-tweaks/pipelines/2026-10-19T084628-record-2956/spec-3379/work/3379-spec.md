# 3379: Merge small adjacent tasks at plan-authoring time

Surface: backend

## Overview

Part of the "Build dispatch cost shape" decomposition (origin #3229). In the measured 10-task build, two implement-and-review cycles would have been saved by combining small adjacent tasks — for example "add a column" followed by "a pure function over it", or "add a job" followed by "remove what it replaces". Each separate task costs an implementer dispatch and a task review even when the second task only consumes the first's output.

This record adds plan-authoring guidance: when two adjacent tasks touch the same files, the second only consumes the first's output, and neither needs its own review surface, the author writes them as one task. This is plan granularity, not SDD's batch rule: it needs no marker, adds no tier or dispatch instruction, and does not interact with `plan-audit.js --count-tasks`' `batched` flag. It sits next to, and cross-references, the fast-lane transcription batching guidance from #3123.

**Complexity:** Low
**Estimated tasks:** 2-3

## Non-Goals

- A new plan marker, a dispatch change, or any change to what `dispatch.md` forwards to SDD.
- Changing the `batched` flag, `--count-tasks`, or the roster row `sdd-whole-branch-review`.
- Re-steering plans already written; the guidance applies to plans authored after it ships.
- Overriding SDD's one-dispatch-per-task default for work that needs its own judgment, its own tests or its own review surface (SDD's "Reserve one-dispatch-per-task" clause stays the rule for those).
