# 4463: reconcile: archive-branches and remote-prune do not accept "branch tip equals or is contained in its merged PR's head" as merged proof

**Related:** #2252

Origin: /claude-tweaks:tidy close-out 2026-10-27 (run 2026-10-27T055458-tidy-standalone)

Defer-reason: tangential

## Current State

After a full reconcile pass on 2026-10-27, `archive-branches` still skipped 97 local branches as `merged-pr-without-cherry-equivalence`, and `remote-prune` skipped 93 remote branches as `not-proven-merged`. Checking the 97 by hand: 62 had a tip exactly equal to their merged PR's `headRefOid`, 28 had a tip that was an ancestor of it, and only 7 carried commits made after the merge. The 90 contained ones were deleted by hand during the tidy close-out. The 7 were tag-archived (`archive/{branch}`) and deleted, matching archive-branches' unmerged-aged treatment.

## Deliverables

Approach: Add the PR-head containment proof next to cherry-equivalence and squash provenance: a MERGED PR whose `headRefOid` equals the branch tip, or has the tip as an ancestor, proves nothing on the branch is unmerged; the commits stay recoverable at `refs/pull/N/head`.

1. Add a PR-head containment proof (tip equal to, or ancestor of, the merged PR's head) to both checks' merged-in-substance evidence.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A branch whose tip equals its merged PR's head is deleted by `archive-branches` (local) and `remote-prune` (remote).

## Release Note

reconcile now cleans up merged branches whose history includes merge commits or several squashed commits.
